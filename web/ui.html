RESEARCH PLAYBOOK
From clean-looking to research-ready.
Make every data decision and information boundary explicit.
METHOD / v1.1
01 · Preserve raw evidence
Transformations create independent snapshots. Synthetic experiments retain the seed, contamination level, instrument, and as-of cutoff. Quarantined rows retain input IDs and failure reasons. Local files stay in browser memory.
02 · Treat time as part of the data
Timestamps refer to bar opening times and use UTC milliseconds at one-minute frequency. Only bars satisfying timestamp + 60s ≤ asOf enter the research dataset. Imported text dates require an explicit timezone.
03 · Keep gaps and questionable prices visible
Require positive prices, nonnegative volume, and valid OHLC bounds. Quarantine invalid rows; do not fill missing prices or invent zero volume. Do not calculate a minute return across a missing bar. MAD flags request review; they neither rewrite prices nor prove that an extreme move is erroneous.
04 · Use only available history
Calibrate anomaly scores on the previous 20-60 returns. Compute risk from 20 prior consecutive valid returns, excluding the current return. Reset risk windows after gaps and anomalies. Lag the signal by one minute. tradable is a data gate, not a fill guarantee or backtest profit claim.
05 · Match the market and research task
This example simulates a continuously traded crypto spot market. Equity calendars, corporate actions, delistings, survivorship bias, versioned adjustments, announcement times, point-in-time joins, order-book sequence numbers, and execution delays need separate treatment.
06 · Reproduce and extend
The research ZIP contains research.ipynb, research.py, shared cleaning.py, input data, dependencies, and instructions. Use Python 3.11+ with Jupyter or VS Code # %% cells. Export any table view as CSV, including quarantine evidence.
A suggested workflow
  • Rebuild a fixed seed and inspect its injected contamination.
  • Walk through code, snapshots, gaps, and quarantine reasons.
  • Change the MAD threshold and compare flagged and data-ready counts.
  • Change the seed or contamination level to explore failure modes.
  • Retain the input, parameters, and as-of cutoff before strategy research.
